NATIVE AEP THUMBNAIL - beta 0.1.0
Real previews for your .aep files, right in Windows Explorer.


WHAT YOU GET
After installing, folders with After Effects projects show what is actually
inside each project - a rendered frame - instead of the plain Ae icon.


HOW IT WORKS
An .aep file contains no picture of any kind, only the project itself. So the
first time a project is seen, After Effects quietly renders one frame of it in
the background. That takes a few seconds; after that the preview is instant
and stays until the project is edited.

Rendering pauses while After Effects is open, so your work is never disturbed.


YOU NEED
- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- Adobe After Effects installed

On the next page you can let setup switch on the After Effects setting
"Allow Scripts to Write Files and Access Network". Rendering previews does
not work without it. Setup keeps a backup of your After Effects preferences
before changing anything.


GOOD TO KNOW
- Previews can be switched off and back on at any time from the Start menu:
  "Turn previews on or off".
- Uninstalling puts everything back exactly as it was.
- If a project's media is on a drive that is not connected, After Effects
  draws its own "media offline" colour bars, and that is what the preview
  shows.
